Consent does not propagate to child frames

pendo.updateCookieConsent() only updates the frame it is called in. Frames that already exist keep their old policy and reject the identity that follows a re-grant; frames created afterwards are still seeded with the tiers the page originally loaded with.

How to reproduce

  1. Load this page. Every frame starts on strictlyNecessary (trackVisitors: false), so all of them collapse to __cookieless_visitor__.
  2. Click Grant identity consent. That calls updateCookieConsent in the top frame only, and schedules a brand-new frame two seconds later.
  3. The top frame fires identifyRequested; this page answers with pendo.identify() and the top frame picks up the real visitor ID.
  4. Check the table: existing frames stay on the sentinel, and the newly added frame is still seeded with the old granted tiers.
